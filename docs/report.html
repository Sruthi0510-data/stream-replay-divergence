The bound, and what it is about

2,633 records are contingent on the batching, which is 8.8% of the log, and they reach 73 output rows.

The bound is correct and tight between replays, which all read the log in offset order. The original run processed in arrival order, and that ordering is not in the log.

The sessions were never replayable

30 combinations of batch size and time per batch, and 0 of them reproduce the original's 205 sessions. The best recovers 21, and matching the original's wall-clock span would need a further 7.2x slowdown.

Meanwhile the drop counter read 8 records at the shipped setting. Nothing would have alerted.
